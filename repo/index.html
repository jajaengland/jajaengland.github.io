Receptor 1.6.0

Safari downloads and file browser for 32-bit iOS 4 through 8; iOS 7-8 support is experimental.

Targets 32-bit iOS 4.0–8.x (below 9.0). Device-to-PC transfers require Receptor Desktop 1.6.0 with receiving enabled. Separate filesystem browsing is read-only and limited by the app’s permissions. New iOS interface and iOS 7–8 adapters need device testing; arm64 Safari is not included. Earlier user reports cover iPad 1 / iOS 5.1.1 and iPad 4 / iOS 6.1.3. IPA launch failures and DEB/device coverage remain incomplete.

Beta — two-way transfers and filesystem browser; iOS 7–8 experimental
